HOW TO PLAY 🎮

⚡ A math question glows on the screen — type the answer and hit Enter (or the ✔ key).
⏱️ Answer before the timer bar runs out — faster answers score more points!
🔥 Correct answers in a row build a streak combo that multiplies your score.
💜 You have 3 lives per level. A wrong or too-slow answer costs one.
⭐ Finish a level to earn up to 3 stars and unlock the next one.
🎓 Start with Practice Mode — no timer, no lives, just learn the ropes!
